About Us

Welcome to De Angels Bar & Grills

De Angels Bar & Grills was created for people who love fire, flavour and good company. We bring together smoky grills, generous local and continental plates, a lively bar and an inviting outdoor space where every visit feels like a celebration.

From pepper chicken and barbecue to rice meals, burgers, small chops and crafted drinks, our kitchen and bar work with one promise: quality food, warm hospitality and moments you will want to repeat.

A Day At De Angels

From the first jollof plate at opening to the last cocktail before dawn — the terrace, grill and bar each have their moment. This is how the house moves through the day.

From 10:00 AM
Afang Soup

Rich afang leaves, prawns and tender protein in a deep Calabar-style broth — a midday favourite for terrace lunches, takeaway and relaxed starts outdoors.

Late Afternoon
Golden Hour

The terrace comes alive — orange seating, green lawn and the grill sign glowing as evening approaches. After-work drinks, small chops and tables filling with familiar faces.

Evening
Grill Night

Fire, smoke and platters built for the table. De Angels Platter, skewers and cocktails land as music rises and the terrace hits its peak.

Until 5:00 AM
Late Hours

Soft bar light, cold pours and pepper soup when the night stretches on. Last orders, long conversations and the kind of evenings New Owerri remembers.
