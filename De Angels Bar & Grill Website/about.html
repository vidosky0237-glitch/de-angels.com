About Us

A Place For Flavour And Company

De Angels Bar & Grills began with a simple idea: serve food that tastes like it came straight off a real fire, pour drinks worth lingering over, and give people a beautiful outdoor space to gather.

Today we are a full hospitality destination — grills, local and continental plates, a lively bar, takeaway, catering and events. Every plate is prepared with care. Every guest is treated like they belong here.

Our Journey

From A Simple Idea To A Full Destination

The Beginning
A fire, a grill, a gathering place

De Angels started with charcoal, generous plates and the belief that people stay longer when the food is honest and the space feels like theirs.

The Terrace
Outdoor living became the heart of the house

The open-air terrace turned meals into evenings — music, conversation, night air and platters made for sharing.

The Kitchen & Bar
Local flavour, continental finish

Jollof, grilled fish and steaks sit beside cocktails, mocktails and ice-cold beers — one menu, one standard.

Today
Dining, events, takeaway and catering

We now host tables, terrace nights, private gatherings and off-site catering — still with the same fire and the same welcome.

The Kitchen

How We Cook

Everything begins with heat, patience and ingredients that can stand up to the grill. We do not hide flavour behind garnish. We let charcoal, spice and time do the work.

Fire first

Charcoal grills, skewers and barbecue smoke — the flavour you taste is the flavour of real fire.

House spice, never rushed

Marinades, yaji and glazes are mixed in-house so every plate carries a De Angels signature.

Plates made for sharing

Mixed grills, small chops and platters are built for tables that talk, laugh and stay.

A bar that matches the kitchen

Cocktails, juices, beers and mocktails are mixed with the same care as the food coming off the grill.
