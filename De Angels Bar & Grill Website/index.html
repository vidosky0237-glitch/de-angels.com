About Us

Welcome to De Angels Bar & Grills

De Angels Bar & Grills was created for people who love fire, flavour and good company. We bring together smoky grills, generous local and continental plates, a lively bar and an inviting outdoor space where every visit feels like a celebration.

From grilled chicken and barbecue to rice meals, burgers, small chops and crafted drinks, our kitchen and bar work with one promise: quality food, warm hospitality and moments you will want to repeat.
