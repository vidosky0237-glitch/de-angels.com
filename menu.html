From The Kitchen

A Few Notes Before You Order

Our grills are cooked to order. Spice levels can be eased or turned up, platters are made for sharing, and the bar will happily pair a drink with whatever comes off the fire.

Spice to taste

Ask for mild, house or extra heat on skewers, asun, wings and pepper soup.

Sharing plates

De Angels platter, small chops and snack boxes are built for the table.

All-day kitchen

Food is served daily from 10:00 AM through late-night hours.
